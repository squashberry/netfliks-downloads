BEFORE YOU INSTALL

Ready to install Netfliks for Android?

Download Netfliks-Android.apk from the button below. If Android blocks the APK, use the troubleshooting steps only when needed.

01

Try “Install anyway” first

When the Blocked by Play Protect warning appears, tap More details (or the small arrow). If Android shows Install anyway, tap it to continue.

02

If Android does not show “Install anyway”

Open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon in the top right, then select Play Protect.

03

Temporarily turn off app scanning

Tap the gear icon in the top right, turn off Scan apps with Play Protect, then tap Turn off to confirm when prompted.

04

Install Netfliks and restore protection

Return to the APK and continue the installation. After Netfliks is installed, turn Scan apps with Play Protect back on.

ⓘ

Android can check apps installed outside Google Play for potentially harmful behavior. Only continue when you trust the download source and have verified that you downloaded the official Netfliks APK.
